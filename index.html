Our Purpose

Every student deserves a safe place to feel heard — without judgment, without barriers, and with the right tools to thrive.

Haven-Circle combines mood tracking, guided journaling, and peer connection to help students turn tough days into moments of growth — anytime, anywhere.

Meet the Founder

Jiaqi (Joshua) Feng

Student Entrepreneur & App Developer

Joshua is a high school student entrepreneur who built Haven-Circle from a deeply personal understanding of student stress and the need for genuine support. Driven by empathy and a passion for technology, he created a platform where every student can feel less alone. Joshua believes that mental wellness tools should be as accessible as the phones in our pockets — and he's making that vision a reality, one feature at a time.
